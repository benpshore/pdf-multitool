## Export observations and limitations

- Text\-layer detection cannot establish whether text was authored natively or generated by earlier OCR\.
- OCR candidates are heuristics; this operation does not run OCR or claim text completeness\.
- Headings, repeated margins and document names are conservative candidates, not verified semantics\.
- Coordinates are PDF points in the unrotated extraction coordinate system; displayed page rotation is reported separately\.
- This export contains observed PDF text\-layer characters, not verified character\-complete content; OCR is not run\.
- Heading levels and reading order are conservative candidates; complex layouts may need review\.
- Only adjacent, similarly styled lines with clear paragraph\-continuation geometry are joined within a source block\.
- Source page boundaries and repeated margins are retained by default; paragraphs are never joined across pages or columns\.
- Line\-end hyphens, citation text and equation characters are preserved rather than reconstructed or corrected\.
- Tables, scientific equations, citations and references are not semantically reconstructed or verified\.
- Vector drawings, annotations, form widgets and original link actions are not reproduced\.
- Source fonts and colors are not reproduced; bold and italic flags are retained where available\.
- Superscript and subscript use safe inline HTML; reader support varies and geometry\-based inferences may be uncertain\.
- Raster images use sibling PNG/JPEG files\. Keep the Markdown file and its generated assets together\.
- Image clipping, masks, placement, rotation and blending may differ from the source\.
