Export observations and limitations
  • Text-layer detection cannot establish whether text was authored natively or generated by earlier OCR.
  • OCR candidates are heuristics; this operation does not run OCR or claim text completeness.
  • Headings, repeated margins and document names are conservative candidates, not verified semantics.
  • Coordinates are PDF points in the unrotated extraction coordinate system; displayed page rotation is reported separately.
  • This is a reflowed text-and-raster-image export, not a pixel-identical PDF rendering.
  • Source fonts are described but not embedded; installed generic fonts may change appearance.
  • Vector drawings, annotations, form widgets and original link actions are not reproduced.
  • Image placement, clipping, masks, rotation and blending may differ from the source page.
  • Reading order uses clear geometric gutters and otherwise preserves parser block order; complex layouts may differ.
  • Superscript uses font flags or conservative baseline geometry; subscript uses geometry and may be uncertain.
  • Repeated headers and footers are retained. Hidden OCR layers can duplicate text visible in an image.
